2. Return Eligibility

A return request may be considered if the product is damaged, defective, incorrect or significantly different from the ordered product.

Products should be returned in their original condition and packaging when applicable.

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact our support team as soon as possible.

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, hygiene requirements or other applicable conditions.

If a refund is approved, the refund process will depend on the order and payment circumstances.

For Cash on Delivery orders, refund arrangements will be discussed with the customer when applicable.

6. Exchange

Product exchange may be available for eligible cases depending on product availability.

Please contact customer support before sending any product back.

7. How to Request a Return

To request a return or refund, contact us through our Contact Us page and provide your order details.

Our support team will guide you through the next steps if your request is eligible.

8. Important Information

Please do not send a product back without contacting our support team first.

Return and refund decisions may depend on the product condition and the specific circumstances of the order.

9. Need Help?

If you have questions about a return or refund, our customer support team is ready to help.
